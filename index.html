The thread

Scroll and watch a single coral thread loop, knot and knit itself into the doll. No cut panels at the start, only a long line finding its shape.

Thread
02

The loop

Every doll is thousands of small loops pulled through each other. We keep the loop tight and even, so the surface stays soft after a hundred cuddles and the ears hold their shape.

A knot is the oldest promise in textiles. It holds because it is pulled, not because it is glued. That is the mark on our label.

03

The cloth

Soft on the first day, sturdier on the hundredth. These are the details we are working to, confirmed on each production run before anything ships.

04

The shape

A round head that melts into a pear-shaped body, two small ears and a little knot on top. It sits up on its own and looks good on a shelf, a bed or a desk.

  • A knot on top, knitted from the last length of thread
  • Embroidered dot eyes and a small curved smile
  • Firm stuffing that keeps the pear shape for years
